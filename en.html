About me

My story

A multidisciplinary professional: I work with strategy, marketing, data and technology.

I started in audiovisual as a freelance advertising scriptwriter and spent four years at a digital marketing agency working with paid and organic traffic and SEO. During my degree in Political Science, I dove into data analysis and programming in Python and R, skills I now apply to building automations, systems and data pipelines.

Over my career I've contributed to advertising campaigns, digital marketing strategies, landing pages, gamification systems and mobilization projects for electoral campaigns, often taking on strategy, copywriting and development within the same project.

I believe that understanding every stage of a project, from conception to delivery, lets me make better-grounded decisions, talk more easily with teams from different specialties and anticipate challenges, even when my own work is focused on a single area.

✳That is the outlook I'm building a path toward project leadership with.
